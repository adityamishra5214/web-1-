The LIVY Ethos
L • I • V • Y

“We do not rush the shuttle. In every gentle thread lies the rhythm of quiet Indian ateliers, where human hands transform raw cotton into sanctuaries of everyday calm.”

LIVY connects India’s living textile lineages with contemporary modern living spaces. Every piece in our Quietly Crafted series is developed in small artisan clusters across Rajasthan and Madhya Pradesh, honoring the tactile integrity of single-origin cotton and zero-chemical botanical dyes.

The Making Process

The Anatomy of Quiet Luxury

Unlike mass-produced synthetic sheets, every LIVY linen passes through 14 pairs of experienced artisan hands before arriving at your doorstep.

1

Single-Origin Long-Staple Cotton

Sourced directly from rain-fed organic farms in Central India, combed to remove short fibers for a pill-resistant, whisper-soft hand feel.

2

Teak Wood Block Engraving

Hand-carved patterns chiseled millimeter by millimeter into aged seasoned teak blocks, creating impressions with soul and character.

3

Fermented Indigo & Madder Vats

Dye baths formulated with natural indigo leaves, jaggery, turmeric, and madder root, producing warm terracotta and deep river indigos.

4

Sun-Bleaching & Water Softening

Air-cured under gentle desert sun and washed in neutral soft water, giving our percale its signature matte, breezy drape.
